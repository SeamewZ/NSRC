NSRC improves all 12 cohort--metric results over fixed DLinear and leads learned baselines in 11/12. Validation selects persistence for both macro cohorts and $0.9\mathbf p+0.1\hat{\mathbf u}^{\,a}_t$ for Celebrity and News, where $\hat{\mathbf u}^{\,a}_t$ is the DLinear forecast with uniformly weighted residual memory. The gain comes from routing: a strong persistence anchor limits transfer when event-conditioned updates do not validate, while a small residual-path contribution helps in the other cohorts. These end-to-end results do not by themselves isolate semantic retrieval; Table~\ref{tab:ablation} tests that mechanism separately.

The identical Macro entries reflect the selected Persistence route: these gains over DLinear come from the anchor, not a residual correction. For Celebrity and News, the selected 90/10 blend makes the calibrated correction a small adjustment to that anchor.
